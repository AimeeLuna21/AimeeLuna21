## Aimee Salazar Luna 

I am a Data Science student on the pre-med track at the University of Virginia with a Spanish minor. My academic and research interests focus on the intersection of data, healthcare, and language accessibility. As a Research Assistant at the **PACT Lab**, I contribute to MindTrails Español, a bilingual mental health app, drawing on both qualitative and quantitative research methods and as a Research Assistant in the **NeuroBioInformatics Lab** where I work with GNNS. 

Beyond research, I am active in leadership and service roles, from alumni relations work in the Inter-Sorority Council to tutoring international staff through UVA’s VISAS program and working as a Spanish Interpreter at UVA Health. These experiences have strengthened my skills in communication, project management, and cross-cultural collaboration.
